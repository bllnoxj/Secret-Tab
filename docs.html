Documentation

Complete reference for Secret Tab: how to use it, how it protects your tabs, and answers to common questions.

How it works

Secret Tab uses local encryption to hide tabs right in your browser. When you hide a tab, its URL, title, and metadata are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored in your browser's local storage. The tab closes immediately, and anyone trying to visit that URL directly will see a fake "offline" page.

To restore a hidden tab, you unlock the extension with your master password. Your password never leaves the browser — it's used to derive an encryption key via PBKDF2, which decrypts your vault.

The encryption flow

  • Hiding: You set a password → derive a 256-bit key (PBKDF2, 310,000 iterations) → encrypt URL + metadata with AES-256-GCM → store in local storage
  • Restoring: You enter your password → derive the same key → decrypt vault → reopen tabs
  • Every operation: Happens in your browser, never sent anywhere

Permissions explained

Secret Tab requires a minimal set of permissions to function. Here's what each one does and why:

tabs

Allows the extension to read the current tab's URL and title when you hide it. Used only to capture what you're hiding.

storage

Stores your encrypted tabs and settings in browser local storage. This is where your vault lives.

scripting

Injects the decoy offline page when someone tries to visit a hidden URL. Shows a fake ERR_INTERNET_DISCONNECTED screen.

webNavigation

Detects when you navigate to a hidden URL so the extension can show the decoy page instead of the real site.

management

Enables anti-disable protection. Prevents anyone from turning off the extension without your password.

Hide a tab

There are three ways to hide the current tab:

  • Popup button: Click the Secret Tab icon and press the Hide button
  • Keyboard shortcut: Press Ctrl+Shift+H (Windows/Linux) or ⌘+Shift+H (Mac)
  • Right-click menu: Right-click any page and select "Hide with Secret Tab"

When you hide a tab, you'll be asked to confirm. You can optionally set a site-specific password for extra protection. After confirming, the tab closes and is moved to your vault.

Restore tabs

To restore a hidden tab:

  1. Click the Secret Tab icon
  2. Click Unlock (if your session has expired)
  3. Enter your master password
  4. Click the tab you want to restore
  5. If the tab has a site password, enter that too

The tab will reopen in a new browser tab. Your vault session remains active for 2 hours, so you won't be asked for your password again unless the session expires.

Vault sessions

After you unlock your vault, a session is created that lasts for 2 hours. During this time, you can:

  • Open and close the extension popup freely
  • Restore multiple tabs without re-entering your password
  • Browse normally

Your session is tied to your browser profile, not the popup window. Closing the popup doesn't lock the vault — only the session timeout does.

You can manually lock your vault at any time by clicking the Lock button in the Settings panel.

Site passwords

For extra security, you can set a unique password for individual tabs. When you hide a tab with a site password:

  • A Perso badge appears next to the tab in your vault
  • You must enter both your master password and the site password to restore it
  • This is useful for particularly sensitive sites or if you want to hide a tab even from someone who knows your master password

Anti-disable protection

By default, anyone with access to your computer can disable Secret Tab from the Extensions page. Anti-disable protection prevents this:

When enabled, you cannot disable Secret Tab without entering your master password. You can still uninstall it, but disabling is blocked at the OS level.

To toggle anti-disable protection:

  1. Click the Secret Tab icon
  2. Open the Settings panel (gear icon)
  3. Toggle "Prevent disabling" on or off
  4. Enter your master password to confirm

Encryption details

Secret Tab uses industry-standard encryption algorithms implemented in the Web Crypto API:

Key Derivation: PBKDF2

Algorithm: PBKDF2-SHA256 with 310,000 iterations. Input: Your master password + a random salt (per device). Output: 256-bit key for AES encryption.

Encryption: AES-256-GCM

Algorithm: AES in Galois/Counter Mode with 256-bit keys. Input: Tab URL + title + favicon + metadata. Output: Ciphertext + authentication tag (prevents tampering).

Salt & IV

A 16-byte salt is generated once per browser profile and stored in local storage. A new 12-byte IV is generated for every encryption operation, preventing identical plaintext from producing identical ciphertext.

Why this is secure

  • AES-256: No known practical attacks. NIST-recommended.
  • GCM mode: Provides both confidentiality and authenticity. Detects if encrypted data has been tampered with.
  • PBKDF2: 310,000 iterations make brute-force attacks computationally expensive. At 1 million guesses/second, a 4-character password would take ~1 week to crack.
  • No server: Your password never travels over the internet. There's no central database to hack.

Keyboard shortcuts

Secret Tab comes with one main keyboard shortcut, and you can customize it:

Action Windows/Linux Mac
Hide current tab Ctrl+Shift+H ⌘+Shift+H

To customize the shortcut:

  1. Go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts
  2. Find Secret Tab in the list
  3. Click in the keyboard input field and press your new shortcut

Change password

Your master password can be changed at any time from the Settings panel:

  1. Click the Secret Tab icon
  2. Click the Settings button (gear icon)
  3. Enter your current password
  4. Enter your new password (min. 4 characters) twice
  5. Click "Update password"

Changing your password re-encrypts your entire vault with the new key. This happens in your browser and takes a few seconds.

Important: If you forget your new password, your vault becomes permanently inaccessible. There is no recovery option.
