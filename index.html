Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for discretion. Every feature is designed to protect your privacy without adding friction.

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Every tab URL and metadata is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being stored locally. Your PBKDF2-derived key never leaves the device.

Fully Offline

No account, no server, no analytics. Secret Tab works entirely in your browser using the Web Crypto API — your data stays on your machine.

Fake Offline Page

Try to visit a hidden URL directly and you'll see a convincing "ERR_INTERNET_DISCONNECTED" page — nothing to reveal what you've hidden.

2-Hour Vault Session

Unlock once, stay unlocked for 2 hours. Your session persists across popup closes, so you're never asked for your password twice in a sitting.

Per-Site Passwords

Add an extra password to individual tabs for even tighter control. The "Perso" badge shows which entries have additional protection.

Anti-Disable Guard

Secret Tab protects itself from being disabled without your password. No one can turn off the extension without going through settings first.

Keyboard Shortcut

Press Ctrl+Shift+H (or ⌘+Shift+H on Mac) to instantly hide the current tab without opening the popup.

Right-Click Menu

Hide any tab from the context menu. Right-click any page and select "Hide with Secret Tab" — no popup interaction needed.

Chromium Compatible

Works on Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, and all Chromium-based browsers. One extension for every browser on your machine.

How it works

Simple to use.
Hard to crack.

Three steps to lock a tab away. Zero steps to get tracked.

01

Set your password

Create a master password (min. 4 characters). Secret Tab derives a 256-bit AES key using PBKDF2 with 310,000 iterations — making brute force impractical.

02

Click "Hide"

Open the popup and press Hide, or use Ctrl+Shift+H. The tab URL, title, and favicon are encrypted and stored. The tab closes immediately.

03

Restore anytime

Unlock your vault with your password. Tabs reopen seamlessly. Your session stays active for 2 hours, so you won't be asked again during a normal browsing session.

04

Lock and forget

Close the popup, lock manually, or wait for the session to expire. Any direct attempt to visit a hidden URL will hit the decoy offline page.
